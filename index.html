Our Story

Powerhouse Roasters began with a Christmas gift from our children — a tiny coffee roaster given to Wes. After long days on our Wyoming farm, evenings were spent roasting small batches and dreaming about serving others. What started quiet became a family-built commitment to honest, dependable coffee.
