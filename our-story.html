Our Story

Built with quiet strength.

Powerhouse Roasters began with a Christmas gift from our children – a tiny coffee roaster given to Wes. In the evenings, after the work on our Wyoming farm was done, Wes would roast small batches of coffee, experimenting with beans and developing roasts for black coffee, lattes, and anything the family requested. As he served each cup and teased his children, he dreamed aloud about roasting coffee for others.

One day, our son joined in the dreaming – and being a big dreamer himself, helped put those ideas into action. What began as quiet evenings around a kitchen roaster became something more – a family-built vision shaped by Wyoming grit, steady hands, and a deep appreciation for honest, good coffee.

We use high-quality beans from some of the best growing regions of the world. Every batch is handcrafted in small quantities for bold flavor and steady reliability. We aren’t trying to be the biggest – just the most dependable part of your day.

Wyoming-roasted coffee. Power in every pour.

Wes & Michelle
Founders, Powerhouse Roasters
