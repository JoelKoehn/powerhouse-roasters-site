A True Wyoming Story

Our Kids Gave Dad a $30 Coffee Roaster for Christmas.

We had no idea it would turn into this. Here's what actually happened.

Keep Reading ↓
One

After long days on our Wyoming farm, Wes started spending evenings roasting small batches on that little Christmas roaster — experimenting, tweaking, serving cups to whoever would drink them.

Two

It wasn't fancy. No warehouse, no big equipment — just a farmer, a small roaster, and a whole lot of trial and error after the real work was done for the day.

"Dad, other people need to taste this."
— Our son, one evening on the farm

That's how Powerhouse Roasters started.

Not in a fancy facility. In our home, on a Wyoming farm, after the work was done. We still roast in small batches. We still watch every single one. The goal is still the same as that first Christmas — make the best cup of coffee we know how to make.
